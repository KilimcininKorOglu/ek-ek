## Why this exists

ek-ek is released under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later,
and is also offered under separate commercial terms
([LICENSE-COMMERCIAL.md](LICENSE-COMMERCIAL.md)). Offering those commercial
terms requires the project owner to hold sufficient rights over the entire
codebase.

A single merged contribution that does not carry these rights removes that
ability permanently, for everyone, and cannot be undone by deleting the code
later. That is why signing is required before any contribution is merged.

## How to sign

Signing happens on the pull request. Open a pull request, and an automated check
will ask you to leave a comment with the exact text it gives you. Your GitHub
username and the date are then recorded, and the check turns green.

You sign once. Later pull requests from the same account do not ask again.
